# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Dolil Onusondhan project welcomes contributors and community members with different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and perspectives.

We are committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and constructive environment for everyone participating in this project's spaces.

This policy applies to project-related interactions, including GitHub issues, pull requests, code reviews, commits, and any official community space maintained for the project.

## Expected behavior

Please:

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions without attacking the people proposing them.
- Give actionable feedback and explain disagreements with evidence where possible.
- Welcome questions from beginners and help others learn.
- Respect different viewpoints, language preferences, and levels of experience.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Protect personal information, credentials, and private land-record data.
- Acknowledge the work and contributions of others.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or deliberate personal attacks.
- Discriminatory, hateful, or demeaning language.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Repeated unwanted contact or deliberate disruption of project discussions.
- Trolling, insults, humiliation, or intentionally hostile review comments.
- Impersonation, deceptive attribution, or knowingly misleading project participants.
- Sharing real deed records, identity documents, account credentials, access tokens, or other private data in issues, pull requests, or test fixtures.
- Encouraging illegal access, privacy violations, or unsafe data collection.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this policy in project spaces.

Maintainers may remove or edit content that violates this policy, close unconstructive discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and focused on maintaining a safe, productive project environment.

## Reporting a concern

Please do not turn a conduct concern involving private information into a public discussion.

Contact the repository maintainer through a contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile:

- [Repository owner profile](https://github.com/jubayerhossaindev)

Include a link to the relevant interaction, a brief description of what happened, and any context needed to understand the concern. Do not publish private messages or personal information without permission.

If you cannot find a suitable private contact method, ask for a private reporting channel without posting sensitive details publicly.

## Scope

This policy applies within project spaces and whenever someone is officially representing Dolil Onusondhan or its maintainers in a project-related public setting.

## Attribution

This is the project-specific Code of Conduct for Dolil Onusondhan. It is informed by common open-source community moderation practices and is not a claim of formal adoption of another organization's policy.
